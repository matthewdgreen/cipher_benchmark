# Beale Papers source note

The Beale records in this unsolved benchmark area are numeric-token transcriptions of Beale ciphers 1 and 3. The canonical provenance used for these records is the public Beale Papers transcription hosted by The Cipher Foundation, which states that its text follows the 1885 pamphlet as represented by an Internet Archive copy.

Decipher does not include Beale cipher 2 as an unsolved challenge here, because cipher 2 has a traditional accepted solution using the Declaration of Independence as a book/key text. Beale 1 and Beale 3 remain without a widely accepted solution.

Local AZdecrypt bundled copies were used as a convenience cross-check for the numeric token streams during import. They are not treated as the primary provenance source.

Primary transcription page: https://cipherfoundation.org/older-ciphers/beale-papers/beale-papers-transcription/
